Last Scout Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

Last Scout is a game for iPhone made by Fernando Amaral. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it works fully offline.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, your progress (salvage, upgrades, crew, achievements and records) and your saved run are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups like any app data. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage data with me. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
