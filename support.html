Last Scout Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: fmfamaral@gmail.com. I reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to move, tap DASH to dodge, and your weapons fire on their own. Each level-up pauses the fight and offers three upgrades. Survive ten minutes, then defeat the district's guardian. The training bay on first launch walks you through it in about a minute; you can replay it from Settings.

My run disappeared

Runs save automatically when you pause or leave the app, and you can resume them from the Hangar. A finished or abandoned run is cleared.

How do I open more levels and crew?

Defeat a level's guardian to open the next level on the district route. Salvage from every run buys Workshop upgrades and brings new crew aboard.

The phone gets warm or the battery drains fast

Choose Settings > Render quality > Battery for a lighter mode at 30 frames per second.

Left-handed play, motion and contrast

Settings has a left-handed layout, reduced motion, high contrast, and separate sound, music and haptics controls.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game is free, plays fully offline, and has no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.
